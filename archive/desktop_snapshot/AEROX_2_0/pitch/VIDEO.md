# AEROX AI CORE 2.0 qualification video

## English script and storyboard

Target 85–105 seconds. Record the team on camera and use their own voices. The separate technical demo is one uninterrupted screen recording.

| Time | Shot | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0–12 s | Team on camera. Project name in one line. | “Drone footage can take time to review, especially when every assignment asks a different question. We built AEROX AI CORE to bring that review into one workspace.” |
| 12–36 s | Actual GUARD screen. Show input provenance, analyze, then select an evidence frame and show the confidence table. | “GUARD applies our experimental RESCUE model to a recorded clip. It marks detected people, reports counts for each analyzed frame, and exports timestamps and evidence for an operator to review. Our internal validation mAP at 0.50 IoU was 38.5 percent.” |
| 36–54 s | Actual AGRI screen. Show the source next to the mask and color grid. | “AGRI measures visible green coverage in an RGB image and compares areas of the field. The grid shows where an operator may want a closer look. Yield prediction still requires validation against measured harvest data.” |
| 54–69 s | Actual FLIGHT screen with its simulation label fully visible. Show checklist, then waypoint progress. | “FLIGHT lets us rehearse a scheduled patrol or a survey in software. The route, battery and link here are simulated.” |
| 69–91 s | Team back on camera. Spare graphic with three module names and project tagline. | “Our FPV engineering experience helps us design around how drone operators work. We want to test review time and reliability with operators in Kazakhstan. Our next steps are independent validation, verified hardware integration and a controlled pilot.” |

If the AGRI sample is a generated test pattern, add “This screen uses a synthetic test pattern with known green coverage” over the AGRI clip or in narration. If GUARD uses the bundled clip, add “VisDrone reference stills assembled into a test clip” and clear media use before publishing. Any AI-generated aerial imagery used as a conceptual shot must carry an on-screen label “AI-generated concept imagery” for its full duration.

## Technical demo recording

- Prepare the 1080p browser view, close unrelated tabs and rehearse once. Keep the module and provenance labels visible. Start the screen recording before the input is selected.
- GUARD · Select the approved input, run analysis, pick an evidence frame and export the report. Keep this AI inference step visible from start to result.
- AGRI · Select an RGB photo or disclosure-labeled fixture, run, and show mask, zones and the yield status.
- FLIGHT · Show its simulation banner, set a scheduled time, complete the checklist and advance along the route.
- Aim for 75–100 seconds. If time is tight, give GUARD priority. End the recording directly after the last interaction. No cuts, acceleration, overlays that obscure the real controls or spliced screens in the technical recording.

## Pitch edit

Use 16:9 with readable English captions. Keep the narration louder than any music, even out team microphone levels, and hold each technical result long enough to read. Cut on spoken phrase boundaries. Check for license and privacy issues in source clips and keep the final technical claims aligned with the AI Card. For uploads, use public-viewable video links and verify both links in a signed-out window. The pitch may be edited and must remain under three minutes. The technical demo must remain unedited and under two minutes.
